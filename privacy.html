Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to how we build and protect Buyhelps

1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy and only collect essential information necessary to provide an exceptional shopping experience:

  • Personal Identity: Your name, email address, phone number, and delivery addresses provided during checkout or account creation.
  • Order History & Preferences: Items purchased, shipping details, saved addresses, wishlist items, and currency/language choices.
  • Payment Security: Payment information is processed via RBI-compliant, PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways. We never store complete credit card or debit card numbers on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for legitimate commercial purposes:

  • To process, dispatch, and track your orders.
  • To send order confirmation notifications, delivery updates, and invoice receipts.
  • To offer personalized customer support via live chat, email, or telephone.
  • To detect, prevent, and protect against fraud and security breaches.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

Buyhelps uses browser LocalStorage and modern cookie technologies to remember your essential preferences—such as your active shopping cart items, saved currency (INR, USD, EUR, etc.), preferred language (English or Hindi), and dark/light display theme. No intrusive tracking cookies are ever deployed.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any external advertisers. We share data only with verified logistical and operational partners necessary for fulfillment:

  • Logistics Partners: BlueDart Express, Delhivery, and India Post for delivering your parcels and sending real-time tracking SMS.
  • Payment Processors: Secure UPI and banking gateways to execute instant authorizations.

5. Data Security

All sensitive information transmitted to and from Buyhelps is encrypted using industry-standard 256-bit Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption. Access to user records is strictly restricted to authorized customer support personnel.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your personal data. At any time, you may:

  • Access or edit your personal profile and saved addresses in your Customer Account portal.
  • Request a full export of your personal purchase history.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and related customer data by contacting privacy@buyhelps.com.

7. Privacy Contact

For inquiries regarding our Privacy Policy or data handling practices, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@buyhelps.com.
